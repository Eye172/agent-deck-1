Privacy & usage analytics.

Agent Deck does not require an account. Your saved shelf stays in your browser. The downloadable IDE skill works offline and contains no analytics code.

What the website measures

When activated, the published website uses Umami Cloud to measure page views and catalogue actions: opening a preset, changing a filter group, opening a comparison, and clicking export, skill, installation-guide or research-material links. Events contain only catalogue IDs, export formats, filter group/action, comparison size or material names.

Page metadata includes the page path, browser language, screen dimensions and referring website origin. Umami processes network requests, including IP address and browser information, to produce approximate visitor, device and country statistics. Agent Deck does not send names, email addresses, search text, URL query strings or fragments, blueprint settings, copied prompts, or your saved shelf. We do not assign user IDs, enable session recording, or add tracking cookies.

Analytics requires a third-party script and sends these limited measurements to Umami. The provider processes the requests under its privacy policy. The selected cloud plan controls retention; Hobby currently retains data for six months.

Your choice

We respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. Local previews and this page do not load the tracker. You can also turn analytics off in this browser. That preference uses local storage and applies to future measurements; clearing browser storage resets it.

How to read the numbers

Visits and estimated visitors are not verified individuals. A download click does not prove a completed download, an installation or subsequent use. Ad blockers, privacy choices, repeat visits and automated traffic affect counts. Collection starts when the tracker is activated; earlier visits cannot be reconstructed.
